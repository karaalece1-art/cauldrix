Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Can Can Studio handles information when you play Cauldrix on iOS or Android, visit this website or contact us.

Data on your device

The game saves levels, currency, lives, spells, tower progress and settings such as language, sound and vibration on your device. It also saves purchase entitlements and hashed transaction references to prevent duplicate delivery. The game has no sign-in or built-in cloud sync and does not send your save to Can Can Studio.

Advertising with Google AdMob

Cauldrix integrates Google Mobile Ads for ads between levels and optional reward videos. The current build requests non-personalized test ads. The SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, ad or app interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Available identifiers depend on your platform and permissions. You can manage advertising and privacy options in your device settings.

  • Google

Stores and purchases

Apple’s App Store and Google Play may provide the store country to help select a language. When purchases are available, the store handles billing and payment details. The game uses transaction information to deliver or restore eligible items and does not receive your card details. Links to other games open the relevant store under its own privacy policy.

  • Apple
  • Google Play

Support and this website

If you email us, we use your email address and the details you send to respond to your request. We retain correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. This site uses no forms, analytics scripts or cookies of its own. GitHub Pages may process technical access data, such as your IP address, as the hosting provider.

  • GitHub

Your choices and data deletion

Resetting progress in Settings clears game progress, while retaining settings and certain purchase records. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data where supported removes the local save. Device backups and store purchase history are managed separately by your platform. To ask about access to or deletion of information you shared with us, contact us using the email below.

Privacy contact

For a privacy question or request, email us. This policy may be updated when our services change.
